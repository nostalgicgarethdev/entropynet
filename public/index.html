// what it does

  

    
[1] nodes commit
Every round, five nodes each lock in a secret number by publishing only its hash. They can't change it later.

    
[2] reveal and mix
On the next tick they reveal. The beacon checks each reveal against its hash, mixes them with a fresh finalized Solana block hash and the previous round.

    
[3] sign and chain
The result is signed with the beacon's ed25519 key and linked to the round before it. Change any round and every later one breaks.

// why launches need it

  

    
no pre-timed snipes
The creator picks a window, like "sometime in the next 10 minutes". A beacon round picks the moment. Nobody knows it in advance, not even the creator.

    
fair first buy
Optional raffle: people sign up before the window opens, and the round that fires draws a winner who gets a head-start buy button. The creator can't pick a friend.

    
entropy receipt
Every launch gets a public receipt naming the rounds that decided it, with the math, so anyone can re-run it.

What is a randomness beacon?
A public service that puts out a fresh random number on a fixed schedule. Everyone sees the same number at the same time, and anyone can check it wasn't rigged.

    
Why can't the beacon cheat?
Each node locks its secret in with a hash one round early, so it can't swap it after seeing the others. A finalized Solana block hash goes in too, which nobody controls. Every round is signed and chained to the last, so history can't be quietly rewritten.

    
Who runs the nodes?
Right now, five nodes run by entropynet. The commit-reveal and the Solana block hash are there so you don't have to take our word for it. Outside node operators are next.

    
How does a fair launch work?
You set a window, say "opens in 5 minutes, lasts 10", connect your wallet and add an image. During the window, every beacon round (every 3 seconds) has a chance to fire. The first round that fires is the launch moment. Nobody can know it in advance, including you. Your page then asks your wallet to sign the pump.fun create, and you have 2 minutes to approve. The receipt shows which round fired and how many seconds after it the coin landed.

    
What's the first-buy raffle?
People drop their wallet before the window opens. The round that fires also draws one winner. When the coin is live, the winner gets a buy button on the receipt that builds a buy for their own wallet to sign and pay. It's a head start, not a lock: pump.fun is open to everyone, so we can't stop others buying at the same time.

    
Are launches real?
Yes. A live launch creates a real coin on pump.fun. Your own wallet signs and pays for it in your browser; entropynet never holds a key or your SOL. Your coin's metadata and image go to IPFS through pump.fun.

    
Who signs and pays?
You do. The server only builds the unsigned transaction and checks it in a simulation. The coin's mint key is made in your browser and never leaves it. Your wallet shows the transaction before you approve.

    
What if I miss the moment?
If the create doesn't land within 2 minutes of the beacon firing, the launch is marked missed. You can still launch on pump.fun yourself, but it won't carry an entropynet receipt.

    
Does it cost anything?
The beacon and the API are free. A launch costs the usual pump.fun rent and network fees, about 0.02 SOL, from your wallet, plus any first buy you add. entropynet takes no fee.

    
Can I use the beacon for my own thing?
Yes. Raffles, giveaways, game seeds, picking winners. Read /api/round/latest and point people to the verifier.
